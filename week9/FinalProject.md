# Vocab a Day

Learn one new word every day and remember it with a photo.

## About
Each day the app gives you a new vocabulary word. You add a picture or photo that reminds you of the word, so every word is tied to a moment in your day. Later you can look back on your words and photos, retake memory tests, and find the places where you saved them.

## Features

### Daily Vocab
- A new word every day
- Add a picture from your library or take a photo with the camera
- Decorate the photo with a filter, text, location, or time

### Calendar
- See each day's word and its photo on a calendar
- Tap a day to look back at that entry

### Map
- See where each photo was taken on a map
- Get a notification when you walk past a place where you took a photo, reminding you of the word you saved there

### Vocab Test
- Take a test on all the words you've learned
- The test unlocks only after you've walked a certain number of steps, because exercise is good for memory

### Multiple Users
- Sign in and keep your own entries
- If you choose to share, other users can see your entries

## Built With
- SwiftUI
- Camera and photo editing (class samples and WWDC "Meet PaperKit")
- JSON for saving entries
- MapKit
- Step counting for unlocking the test
- Firebase for multiple users
